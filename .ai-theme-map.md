## 2. Dynamic Theme State, Registry & Context
- `src/themes/index.ts` — Central registry exporting `THEME_ENGINES`, `AVAILABLE_THEMES`, and `normalizeThemeId()`.
- `src/themes/types.ts` — TypeScript definitions for `CanonicalThemeId`, `ThemeEngineDefinition`, `AccentColorId`.
- `src/context/InterfaceStyleContext.tsx` — Manages runtime theme selection, `localStorage` persistence, accent seed switching, ambient glow intensity, glass strength, and **Aero Palette** (`aeroPalette: 'ink' | 'azure' | 'emerald'`, `setAeroPalette`, `aeroAccentColor`).
- `src/utils/themes.ts` — DOM attribute syncing (`data-theme`, `data-interface-style`, `data-aero-palette`).

---

## 3. Theme Studio UI & Exclusions
- `src/components/SchedulesDonateView.tsx` (under `Themes` sub-tab):
  - Interface style selector cards (`Aero UI`, `Fluent Glass`, `Aetherial Focus`).
  - **Aero UI Specifics**:
    - **Aero Palette Selector**: 3 reactive options (`Ink` `#0f172a`, `Azure` `#0284C7`, `Emerald` `#059669`) driving `--aero-palette-color` and `--accent-primary`.
    - **Glass Controls Hidden**: Live Glass Optics, Ambient Glow, Glass Strength, Ambient Drift, GPU Safeguard, and Glass Surface Engine are cleanly hidden when `isAero` is active.
    - **Community Theme Mods Hidden**: Custom stylesheets & verified community mods card is hidden when `isAero` is active to maintain strict visual purity.
    - **Target FPS Selector**: Remains accessible across all themes.
- `src/mods/themes.json` — Bundled community theme presets (active only in dark themes).
- `src/utils/themeMods.ts` — Loads, parses, and injects community theme mods.

## 4. Shared Design System Primitives
- `src/components/LiquidDock.tsx` — Bottom navigation bar (`Focus`, `Insights`, `ZeroTube`, `Settings & OS`, `Support`). In Aero UI, renders as a clean `#FFFFFF` bar with `#F1F5F9` border.
- `src/components/GlassCard.tsx` — Glass card container responding to active theme tokens. In Aero, becomes a frosted white card.
- `src/components/ObsidianCard.tsx` — Deep elevated surface container responding dynamically to dark and light modes. In Aero, renders on `#F6F9FC` / `#FFFFFF` with `#CBD5E1` borders.
- `src/components/AmbientBackground.tsx` — Sub-surface glowing ambient mesh orbs (hidden/minimized in Aero UI).
- `src/components/EscapeTicketSystem.tsx` — Escape Ticket dock button & modal. In Aero UI, renders a pure white ticket (`#FFFFFF`) with bold typography, prominent count indicators, and high-contrast badge.
