Bag switching (`Work`, `Commute`, `Study`, `Family`), bag rename/customization, essential communication toggles, and installed app picker. Adapted for Aero UI with squircle capsules (`rounded-2xl`), high-contrast matte black typography (`#0f172a`), pure white cards with `#CBD5E1` borders, and palette-tinted action buttons.
